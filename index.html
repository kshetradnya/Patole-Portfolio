About
Infrastructure doesn't fail at the construction stage. It fails at the engineering table. Get the decisions right there, and everything else follows.
Vivek Patole, Vice President of Engineering

I started as a Trainee Engineer at a small consultancy in Mumbai. Three decades later I lead central engineering for some of India's largest EPC organisations, and the question I find most interesting hasn't changed: how do you deliver complex infrastructure on time, on budget, and to a standard that lasts 100 years?

My work spans civil, structural, architectural and HVAC engineering across hydrocarbon, power, railway, water and buildings, from Pre-FEED through construction support, including brownfield and debottlenecking projects. I've led discipline teams at Chemtex, Toyo Engineering, Worley, KEC International and Tata Projects.
